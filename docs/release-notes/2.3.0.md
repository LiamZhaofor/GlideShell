2.3 可以在总览里直接调整虚拟桌面的顺序了。这是 GlideShell 收到的第一个外部贡献，感谢 [CBalaa](https://github.com/CBalaa)（#2）。

## 拖动调整桌面顺序

- 三指上滑打开总览，把指针移到顶部展开桌面条，按住一个桌面的缩略图左右拖，其他桌面会顺滑地让开位置。
- 松手后缩略图飞进新位置，Windows 里的桌面顺序也跟着改：任务视图、Ctrl+Win+←/→、四指左右滑都按新顺序走。
- 拖回原来的位置再松手，顺序不变。系统没能调整顺序时，缩略图会飞回原位。
- 松手后马上做三指手势也不用等动画播完。

## 修正与改进

- **电脑睡眠唤醒后三指轻点中键失灵**：Windows 有时会在唤醒后一直拒绝长期运行的程序发出的点击，Windows 自带的三指轻点中键也会这样失灵。现在 GlideShell 发现点击发不出去时会换一种方式代发；确认是这个进程被系统拒绝了，会等空闲时自动重启一次来恢复。鼠标按键手势的单击补发也一样。「设置 → 诊断」里能看到代发了几次。

## 安装与升级

- 已装 2.0.0 – 2.2.1 的：点托盘的升级通知或「设置 → 关于 → 升级」，一键升级，设置保留。
- 新安装：下载 `GlideShell-2.3.0-win-x64.zip`，解压后双击 `Install.cmd`（不需要管理员权限）。
- 完整的使用教程：<https://liamzhaofor.github.io/GlideShell/guide.html>

## 已知限制

- 只在一台机器上完整验证过（ASUS 精确式触摸板，并行报告模式）。在别的触摸板上遇到问题，请附上「设置 → 诊断」生成的诊断报告。
- 程序没有正式的代码签名，第一次运行时 Windows 可能提示「无法验证发布者」。
- 普通权限版在以管理员身份运行的程序在前台时收不到按键（Alt+Tab、Alt+Space 交给 Windows），中键也传不到它。

## English

2.3 lets you reorder virtual desktops right in the overview. This is GlideShell's first outside contribution. Thanks to [CBalaa](https://github.com/CBalaa) (#2).

**Reorder desktops by dragging**
- Swipe up with three fingers to open the overview and move the pointer to the top to expand the desktop strip. Press and drag a desktop thumbnail left or right, and the other desktops smoothly make room.
- When you let go, the thumbnail flies into its new place and Windows' own desktop order changes too, so Task View, Ctrl+Win+←/→ and four-finger swipes all follow the new order.
- Drop it back where it was to keep the order. If Windows can't reorder the desktops, the thumbnail flies back.
- Three-finger gestures right after a drop don't wait for the animation to finish.

**Fixes and improvements**
- **Three-finger tap stopped middle-clicking after the PC woke from sleep.** After waking, Windows sometimes keeps rejecting clicks sent by long-running programs; Windows' own three-finger tap fails the same way. GlideShell now sends the click another way when it can't get through, and if Windows is rejecting the whole process, it restarts itself once when idle. Clicks re-sent by mouse button gestures are covered too. Settings → Diagnostics shows how often this happened.

**Install or upgrade**
- On 2.0.0 – 2.2.1: click the update notification or Settings → About → Upgrade. Your settings are kept.
- New install: download `GlideShell-2.3.0-win-x64.zip`, extract it and double-click `Install.cmd` (no administrator rights needed).

**Known limitations**: only fully verified on one ASUS precision touchpad; not code-signed, so Windows may warn about an unverified publisher on first run; the standard build can't send keys or middle clicks to apps running as administrator.
